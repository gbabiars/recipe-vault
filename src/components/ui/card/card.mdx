# Card

## Purpose and choice

Groups related content on a surface. Use Chip for one compact tag and a plain semantic container when no surface is needed.

## Variants and composition

variant is default or subtle; padding is small, medium, large, or none. Use as for section or li semantics. A whole-card primary link needs `href` and `label`; it uses the app's configured link renderer, or a native anchor without a provider. Use `render` with `label` for a primary button or an individual link override. Stack, Inline, Heading, and Text can structure its contents.

## Content and accessibility

Keep a visible heading or equivalent context. The interactive label names its primary action; nested controls remain separately interactive.
